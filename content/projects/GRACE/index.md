GRACE aimed to equip European law enforcement agencies with advanced analytical and investigative capabilities to respond to the spread of online child sexual exploitation material. 

Within GRACE, I developed multilingual speech recognition and keyword spotting technologies for law enforcement, handling large-scale multilingual datasets and adapting models to unseen domains.
