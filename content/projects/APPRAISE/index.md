Ensuring public spaces' safety while preserving the freedom of citizens represents a significant challenge for European societies. Soft targets like malls, stadiums, or big events continue to face a variety of evolving cyber and physical threats. APPRAISE builds on the latest advances in big data analysis, artificial intelligence, and advanced visualisation by creating a robust security framework that improves both the cyber and physical security of these public spaces.

As a researcher in APPRAISE, I adapted speech recognition tools for threat intelligence in online content, focusing on scalable pipelines for multilingual data and robust performance in real-world conditions.


### Project Overview

The APPRAISE project provides an integrated threat intelligence solution designed to offer unprecedented capabilities to predict and identify criminal and terrorist acts, while enhancing private-public collaboration among security actors. 

The framework is designed to support secured private-public collaboration, optimising the coordination of operations involving private security staff, private operators, and law enforcement agencies. The APPRAISE system and its tools were demonstrated across four complementary pilot sites:
1. An international tennis tournament in Italy.
2. A transnational cycling tour with stages in France and Spain.
3. An international fair in Poland.
4. A large shopping mall in Slovenia.

The APPRAISE consortium brought together 27 partners of excellence from 9 countries, bridging the gap between technological innovation and operational law enforcement. Key partners included:
