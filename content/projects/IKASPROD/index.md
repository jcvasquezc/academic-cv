The **IKASprod** project (*Tecnologías avanzadas para el aprendizaje (IKASI) por demostración en entornos productivos inteligentes*) addresses the development of intuitive, natural interaction technologies to enable human operators to instruct and program collaborative industrial robots without writing manual code. By combining multimodal sensory streams, including speech, computer vision, and mixed reality, the project provides flexible, human-centered programming by demonstration to accelerate industrial reconfigurability in Industry 5.0.

As a speech and multimodal AI researcher at Vicomtech, I was responsible for designing and developing the **multimodal speech recognition architecture for noisy factory floors (Work Package 1)**. In collaboration with Tecnalia, we built a hybrid architecture that pairs acoustic automatic speech recognition (ASR) with visual speech recognition (VSR / lip-reading) and uses an instruction-tuned Large Language Model (LLM) as a semantic arbitrator to infer correct robot commands under severe industrial acoustic noise.

### The Challenge: Natural Language Interaction on High-Noise Factory Floors

Deploying speech interfaces in manufacturing environments is severely hindered by extreme non-stationary acoustic noise generated by heavy machinery, CNC milling, and background operations. When the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) drops to 0 dB or below, acoustic cues are lost, causing unimodal ASR systems to suffer from severe Word Error Rate (WER) degradation and command failures. 

To overcome this fundamental physical boundary, our work introduced an audiovisual framework that leverages visual articulation from camera feeds (which are invariant to acoustic noise) alongside acoustic signals.


### Industrial Demonstrators & Use Cases

The technologies developed across IKASprod were deployed and validated on physical hardware within two manufacturing cells hosted at IMH Campus:

1. **Autonomous Machine Feeding (CNC Milling):** An autonomous mobile manipulator (an industrial robotic arm mounted on an autonomous mobile base) that feeds raw workpieces and switches cutting tools across multiple machining centers, guided by natural human instructions and virtual demonstration.
2. **Intralogistics and Sensorized Tool Storage:** An autonomous mobile robot (AMR) integrated with an automated storage cabinet to pick, deliver, and organize tooling parts based on verbal instructions and high-level behavioral directives.

### Consortium Partners

The IKASprod consortium brings together technology centers, university research units, and industrial training campuses across the Basque Research and Technology Alliance (BRTA):

* **Tekniker** (Project Coordinator - Mixed reality environments, task-oriented dialogue, and robot programming by demonstration)
* **Vicomtech** (Robust acoustic speech recognition)
* **Tecnalia** (Visual speech recognition, computer vision, 6D pose estimation)
* **ORAI NLP Teknologiak** (Natural Language to Code, translation of instructions into Behavior Trees)
* **IMH Campus** (Advanced manufacturing cells, industrial machine-feeding, and warehouse use cases)
